DuckGoDo privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

DuckGoDo is a personal habit tracker made and used by one person for her own habits. This page explains what the app does with data.

What the app uses

When you sign in with Google, DuckGoDo asks for one permission only: access to its own hidden application data folder in your Google Drive. It cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive.

What is stored there

Small files that keep a laptop and a phone in step: the habits, food entries, schedule ticks and notes you enter in the app. They are stored in your own Google Drive, in your own account.

Who can see it

Only you. The app has no server of its own and no database that belongs to anyone else. The phone page holds no keys or secrets. Your data is not sent to the developer, is not sold, is not shared, and is not used for advertising or analytics.

Cookies and tracking

The phone page keeps its working copy of your data in your own browser's storage. There are no trackers.

Removing your data

You can stop the app at any time by removing DuckGoDo under Third-party access in your Google Account security settings. You can also delete the app's hidden folder data from your Google Drive settings under Manage apps.
